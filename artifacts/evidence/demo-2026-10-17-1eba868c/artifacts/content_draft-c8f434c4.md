# Newsletter: Your broken blender? We can fix that.

Dear neighbour,

Did you know that 37% of items brought to our repair cafes are small appliances? And 61% of everything that comes through the door gets fully repaired on the day. That's a lot of blenders, kettles, and toasters saved from the bin.

We're also learning what helps our volunteers thrive. Pairing newcomers with experienced fixers is key. And when sessions are well-staffed, burnout drops.

Come see us at the next cafe. Bring something broken, leave with something fixed.
