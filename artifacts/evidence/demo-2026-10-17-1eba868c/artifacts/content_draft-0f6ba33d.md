# LinkedIn: How repair cafes are fixing more than just broken toasters

Last year, our local repair cafes saw 37% of items brought in being small appliances. Volunteers successfully repaired 61% of all items on the spot. 

For residents, the top reason to visit was saving money (54%), followed by reducing waste (33%). For volunteers, pairing with experienced fixers made all the difference in building confidence and skills. 

We're working with local councils to expand these sessions. If you've been thinking about starting or supporting a repair cafe, now is the time. Let's fix things together.
